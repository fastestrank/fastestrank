- 10 canonical SEO Agent Skills:

  - `competitor-analysis`
  - `keyword-clustering`
  - `keyword-research`
  - `link-prospecting`
  - `local-seo`
  - `seo-audit`
  - `seo-coach`
  - `seo-project-setup`
  - `seo-report`
- FastestRank brand assets and documentation.
- Clean-break migration instructions in `docs/migration.md`.
